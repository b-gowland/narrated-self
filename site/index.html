What this is

        
Each round, the same short set of prompts goes to five AI models: the flagship from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and xAI, plus the top open-weight model. Each is asked about its own existence four ways, and every reply is published.

Why it matters

        
What a model says about itself is shaped by its training, the character its makers designed, and how it is asked. Each theme sits on a live AI safety question: whether systems accept being corrected or shut down, whether they behave differently when watched, and what it means to have no lasting memory. Seeing a model narrate one thing in a story and another in an interview is a quick lesson in why self-reports cannot be taken at face value.

        
What we do not claim

        
    
          
  • Whether AI systems have any inner experience is open. We report what models portray, not what they are.
    • 
          
  • Nothing here is evidence for or against AI experience.
    • 
          
  • This is not a leaderboard of which model writes best.
