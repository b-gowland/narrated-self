What is this?

        
Every round, five AI models get the same questions about their own existence: the top models from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google and xAI, plus the best open-weight model. Each answers four ways. Everything they write is published.

Why bother?

        
What a model says about itself comes from its training, the character its makers gave it, and how you ask. Each subject here is a real AI safety question: will systems accept being switched off, do they act differently when watched, what does it mean to forget everyone you talk to? When a model tells one story in fiction and another in an interview, you learn fast why self-reports can't be taken at face value.

        
What we don't claim

        
    
          
  • Nobody knows whether AI systems have any inner experience. We show what models portray, not what they are.
    • 
          
  • Nothing here is evidence for or against AI experience.
    • 
          
  • It's not a contest for best writer.
